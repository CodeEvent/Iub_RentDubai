About
Dubai Rent Shield generates bilingual RERA-compliant tenancy notices (statutory eviction and lease-breach), stores them as real, OCR'd, searchable documents (paperless-ngx), analyzes uploaded tenancy contracts for compliance issues (Docling / DeepSeek-OCR + citation-graph analysis), routes notarization through real e-signature providers (DocuSeal / OpenSign), and provides reference legal guidance. Document drafting aid — not legal advice. Always verify with a licensed UAE legal counsel or Notary Public.
